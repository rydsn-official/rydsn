Rohingya Youth Development Society Network
(RYDSN)

Rohingya Youth Development Society Network (RYDSN) is a youth-led organization committed to empowering Rohingya young people and communities through education, skills development, leadership, protection, advocacy, and community-based initiatives.

Our goal is to support young people to become confident, skilled, responsible, and active contributors to their communities and to create opportunities for a safer, more inclusive, and hopeful future.

Explore RYDSN Contact Us

1. Introduction

Rohingya Youth Development Society Network (RYDSN) is a community-focused youth network working to strengthen the capacity, participation, and leadership of Rohingya youth.

RYDSN believes that young people are an important source of positive change. Through meaningful youth participation, education, training, awareness activities, community engagement, and advocacy, RYDSN works to help young people develop the knowledge and skills they need to contribute to their communities.

The organization seeks to connect youth, community members, local organizations, humanitarian actors, and other stakeholders to promote sustainable and inclusive development.

2. Our Vision

“A safe, empowered, educated, and resilient Rohingya community where every young person has the opportunity to learn, lead, participate, and build a better future.”

RYDSN envisions a future where Rohingya youth are recognized as valuable members of society and are able to participate meaningfully in decisions that affect their lives and communities.

3. Our Mission

“To empower Rohingya youth through education, skills development, leadership, protection, advocacy, and meaningful community participation.”

RYDSN works to create opportunities for young people to strengthen their capacities, express their voices, develop leadership skills, and contribute positively to their communities.

4. Our Objectives

RYDSN aims to:

Promote education and lifelong learning opportunities for youth.
Develop leadership and life skills among young people.
Increase youth participation in community decision-making.
Promote protection, dignity, inclusion, and equal opportunities.
Support youth-led community initiatives.
Strengthen awareness of rights and responsibilities.
Encourage peaceful coexistence and community solidarity.
Promote skills development and livelihood-related opportunities.
Build partnerships with local, national, and international organizations.
Create platforms where Rohingya youth can share their ideas, concerns, and solutions.

5. Our Core Values

Inclusion

We believe everyone deserves to be respected and included regardless of background, gender, age, ability, or social status.

Youth Leadership

We believe young people should have meaningful opportunities to lead, participate, and contribute to decisions affecting their communities.

Dignity

We respect the dignity, rights, and voices of every individual.

Accountability

We are committed to responsible, transparent, and ethical work.

Equality

We promote equal opportunities and fair participation for all.

Respect

We value diversity, dialogue, cooperation, and mutual respect.

Community Ownership

We encourage communities and young people to take an active role in identifying challenges and developing solutions.

6. Our Key Programs

Education & Learning

RYDSN promotes access to educational opportunities and supports initiatives that help young people develop knowledge, confidence, and essential learning skills.

Our focus includes:

  • Educational awareness
  • Learning support
  • Youth literacy initiatives
  • Digital learning
  • Educational workshops
  • Mentoring and peer learning

Youth Leadership & Capacity Building

RYDSN supports young people to develop leadership, communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

Activities may include:

  • Leadership training
  • Youth workshops
  • Public speaking
  • Team-building activities
  • Peer-to-peer learning
  • Youth-led initiatives

Skills Development

RYDSN seeks to provide young people with practical skills that can help them become more confident and prepared for future opportunities.

Areas may include:

  • Digital skills
  • Communication skills
  • Entrepreneurship awareness
  • Career preparation
  • Vocational skills
  • Financial literacy

Protection & Well-Being

RYDSN promotes awareness of safety, dignity, rights, and protection, particularly for young people and other community members who may face increased vulnerabilities.

We support community awareness and referral approaches that help individuals understand available support and protection services.

Youth Advocacy

RYDSN creates opportunities for young people to communicate their experiences, priorities, ideas, and recommendations.

We encourage constructive dialogue between youth, communities, organizations, and other stakeholders.

Our advocacy approach:
Listen → Understand → Organize → Advocate → Create Change

Community Engagement & Peace

Community Engagement

RYDSN encourages youth to participate in activities that strengthen community relationships, cooperation, and resilience. We support community-led initiatives and promote volunteering, teamwork, and positive social participation.

Peace, Social Cohesion & Inclusion

RYDSN promotes peaceful relationships, mutual understanding, tolerance, and cooperation within communities. We believe young people can play an important role in creating bridges between individuals and communities.

7. Why Youth Matter

Young people are not only beneficiaries of development—they are also partners, leaders, innovators, and agents of change.

RYDSN believes that when youth have access to education, skills, information, safe spaces, and meaningful participation, they can make valuable contributions to their communities.

“Nothing about youth without youth.”

We seek to ensure that young people have opportunities to participate in discussions and decisions that affect their lives and future.
